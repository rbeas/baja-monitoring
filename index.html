Six programs have monitored kelp forests here since 2006, but only MexCal / MexCal–Reef Check Baja sites are on the map. GEM's 16 sites (2011–13), the COBI/Stanford community sites (2006–20) and MásKelp's sites (2019–) are still missing. To put your sites on the WSN poster, send one row per site with:

  1. Program name and who does the surveys (academics, citizen scientists, fishing cooperatives, NGO, government)
  2. Site name and approximate coordinates
  3. Years surveyed (a range is fine)
  4. Any merger, split or hand-over your program went through, and when

One template for everyone: WSN2026_monitoring_coverage_template (tabs: programs · lineage · coverage). Contact: R. Beas-Luna (UABC) · J.A. Garcia-Pantoja (Reef Check).
